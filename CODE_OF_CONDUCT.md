# Code of Conduct

BUI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be respectful, welcoming and patient.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ppmpreetham/BUI/security/advisories/new)
with "Code of Conduct" in the title, or by contacting a maintainer directly.
All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove
contributions or ban contributors who break these rules.
